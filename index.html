The Problem

The challenge isn't craftsmanship. It's the infrastructure around it.

01

No Brand Identity

Artisans produce exceptional work with no brand of their own to carry its value to market.

02

Middlemen Dominate

Layers of intermediaries capture most of the margin before it reaches the artisan.

03

Underutilized Government Schemes

Support like PM Vishwakarma, PMEGP and MUDRA exists but stays largely inaccessible.

04

Limited Digital Literacy

Without digital skills, artisans can't participate in modern commerce or marketing.

05

Low & Stagnant Income

Fragmented access to buyers and capital keeps incomes flat year after year.

06

Finance & Export Barriers

Lack of formal identity blocks access to credit, insurance and export channels.
